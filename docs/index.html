STAG the stag hunt coin

Every round, holders can lock STAG into a hunt. If the hunters together lock enough of all STAG, every hunter gets their stake back plus a bonus. If they fall short, every hunter loses a slice, which is burned. Supply grows when the community coordinates and shrinks when it doesn't.
